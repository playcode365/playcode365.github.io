We use this information only for providing game features, improving the service and statistics, answering support requests.

3. How long we keep it

When you delete your account we delete your personal information without delay, except records the law requires us to keep:

We do not give your personal information to third parties without your consent or a legal basis. To run the Service we use the following providers:

  • After a 7-day grace period we delete the data without delay, in a way that cannot be recovered.
