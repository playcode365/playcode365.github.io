About the game

The horde is coming. Hold the barrier before it breaks!

Siren Defense is a four-lane defense game you play with one finger. Drag left and right to move the heroine Aria, and gun down the monsters marching single file down four lanes.

■ CONTROLS THAT FOLLOW YOUR FINGER
· Aria tracks your finger one-to-one.
· Bullets fly straight ahead. Which lane to cover right now is your call.

■ A NEW BUILD EVERY RUN
· Each level-up, pick one of three cards: multishot, bullet storms, turrets and more.
· Meet a card's conditions and it evolves, turning the fight around in an instant.
· Grab reward gates on the battlefield for extra level-ups and luck.

■ TURRETS THAT GUARD THEIR LANE
· Build turrets on the lanes to fight alongside Aria.
· Each turret only targets its own lane, so where you build matters.

■ BOSSES THAT TAKE A LANE
· When a boss appears, its lane becomes its stage. Defend the other lanes while you bring it down.

■ BUSY? GO AUTO
· A smart auto battle moves between lanes and fights for you.
· Sweep cleared stages to collect rewards fast.

■ GEAR AND MERGING
· Merge three of the same gear to raise its grade and stars. Auto-merge tidies everything up at once.

■ SEASON PASS AND SKINS
· Every season brings new rewards and a new skin for Aria.
